Library

Libraries are important cornerstones of a healthy community. Libraries give people the opportunity to find jobs, explore research, experience new ideas, get lost in wonderful stories, while at the same time providing a sense of place for gathering. Our library is a unique and valuable resource. It is a lifeline to the world and all the information in it. Situated on the ground floor of the Polytechnic building, Hand-picked books line the cupboards to supplement lectures of all courses offered in our Institute. The Library is replete with special interest magazines, References books, industry periodicals and general interest newspapers. The library stands apart with its open access system. The Library also houses master files of examination papers that serve as ready reckoners for the students. To ensure optimal use of the library, a Library Orientation Programme is especially conducted for all students at the beginning of every academic year.

Library Rules

1.Silence should be observed strictly in the Library.

2.All the students should produce their identity card at the entrance.

3.All the students should enter their name in the Entry Register.

4.Before entering in to the library students should switch off the cell phones.

5.Journals will not be issued; but are available for study within the Library.

6.Tearing of sheets from books and newspapers is strictly prohibited and is punishable.

7.Students must satisfy themselves whether the books are in good condition, before they leave the counter. If any damage is noticed, the same must be immediately intimated. Otherwise they will be held responsible for any damage.

8.Loss of borrowing cards and books should be reported to Librarian immediately.

9.Duplicate borrowing cards will be issued on payment of Rs.50/-

10.Using Cellular phones and audio instruments with or without speaker or headphone is strictly prohibited in the library premises.
