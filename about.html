About Trust

Shri Yashwantrao Bhonsale Education Society (SYBES) is a premier educational organization in Sindhudurg district established in 2002 with the only aim to make rapid progress of the students through high quality, diversified and multidisciplinary education which will help the young ones to meet the challenges of 21stcentury and to capitalize their skills ahead. Shri Achyut Sawantbhonsale, a visionary & dynamic leader had laid the foundation of SYBES campus at Charathe, Sawantwadi in 2013. Spread across 16 acres, SYBES campus is situated amidst idyllic hills & lush green ambience to increase the harmony between study and nature. To cater the need of technical & professional education in Konkan region especially in Sindhudurg district, SYBES started Yashwantrao Bhonsale Polytechnic with a state of art ultra-modern infrastructure in 2014 offering Civil, Mechanical & Electrical diploma engineering courses. After the successful commencement of Polytechnic, SYBES immediately established the first ever degree college of pharmacy in Sindhudurg, Yashwantrao Bhonsale College of Pharmacy in 2015. This back to back initiative brought a huge revolution in the educational scenario of Sindhudurg district as the aspirant rural students were striving for such kind of value added curriculum. Both the institutes of SYBES having all types of facilities like Hostel, Mess, Canteen, Sports Ground, Gym, ATM & Transportation are functioning very well. In a very short span Shri Yashwantrao Bhonsale Education Society has gained a very high reputation and public faith for its honest & transparent management. It has earned people’s trust that every initiative taken by the society will be in favour of people and future generation only. The society has taken a huge stride towards providing the advance education to each & every rural student of the region. Continuing the same zeal, SYBES is all set to expand its wings by setting up new educational institutes which will help the students to reach new heights in the world of global opportunities.
